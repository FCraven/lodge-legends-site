The Problem

Every lodge has a story. Almost none are telling it.

Membership everywhere depends on good men discovering the Craft — yet most lodge pages post a meeting flyer a few times a year, and the loudest voices about Freemasonry online are the conspiracy channels. The lodges that thrive in the next decade will be the ones whose story is public, accurate, and beautifully told. That takes editorial discipline most volunteer officers don't have hours for. That's the part we built a machine for — and the taste we bring ourselves.

The Work · Start to Launch

From first call to first post in three weeks

  1. Sit-down

    A call with your Master, Secretary, or social officer: what your lodge is proud of, what it's comfortable sharing, and who holds the keys. We set the privacy line together — it's yours, not ours.

  2. Research & design

    We build your launch library — every figure verified, every image licensed — and design your feed and page to your lodge's character. You review everything in the preview queue.

  3. The one human step

    Your admin authorizes the Instagram connection in a guided fifteen minutes — we never touch your password, by policy. Credentials stay yours; you can revoke ours any time.

  4. It runs

    Posts publish on rhythm. Your officers edit anything, any time, up to two weeks ahead — or touch nothing at all. You get a monthly account of what the feed brought in.

Our Undertaking

The standard we won't trade

Documented, not folklore. Private things stay private.

We will not publish an unverified membership claim, an unsourced quotation, or an unlicensed image — the audience includes brethren who know the history, and your lodge's name stands over every post. And nothing esoteric is ever part of the work: no ritual, no modes of recognition. Anything that even approaches ceremony goes to your officers for review before it airs.

If we wouldn't stand behind a post in open lodge, it doesn't publish.
